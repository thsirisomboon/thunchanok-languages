# 🧭 CEFR Learning Path

The learning system follows a progressive path from foundational communication to advanced language mastery.

### 🌱 A1 — Foundation

**Build the basics.**

Basic vocabulary, pronunciation, greetings, introductions, essential grammar, and survival communication.

---

### 🌿 A2 — Everyday Communication

**Become comfortable with daily life.**

Daily routines, common situations, practical conversations, descriptions, and frequently used language.

---

### 🌳 B1 — Independent User

**Express yourself independently.**

Narrate experiences, explain ideas, express opinions, give reasons, and communicate in connected speech.

---

### 🌲 B2 — Confident Communication

**Discuss ideas with greater precision.**

Argumentation, discussion, abstract topics, complex grammar, nuanced vocabulary, and extended communication.

---

### 🎓 C1 — Advanced & Professional

**Use language with flexibility and precision.**

Academic language, professional communication, sophisticated vocabulary, idiomatic expression, register, and nuance.

---

### 🏛️ C2 — Mastery

**Understand and express subtle meaning.**

Precision, flexibility, idiomatic language, sophisticated discourse, register, stylistic variation, and near-native control.

---

# 🧠 How I Learn

My learning system moves from **recognition → understanding → production → real-world use**.

```text
🧠 Vocabulary
       ↓
🔗 Chunks & Collocations
       ↓
💬 Sentences
       ↓
🧩 Grammar
       ↓
🗣️ Conversation
       ↓
🎧 Listening & Shadowing
       ↓
✍️ Production
       ↓
🌍 Real-World Use
       ↓
🔄 Review
```

The goal is not simply to memorise individual words.

The goal is to build **usable language**.

---

# 📚 My Learning Library

### 🧠 Vocabulary

Build a strong vocabulary base with pronunciation, meaning, parts of speech, examples, synonyms, antonyms, and usage notes.

---

### 🔗 Chunks & Collocations

Learn words in the combinations in which they naturally occur.

> **Vocabulary → Chunk → Sentence → Communication**

---

### 💬 Key Sentences

Study complete sentences that demonstrate how vocabulary, grammar, and natural expression work together.

---

### 🧩 Grammar

Understand grammar through patterns, examples, sentence structures, and real usage rather than isolated rules.

---

### 🗣️ Conversation

Turn knowledge into communication through realistic dialogues and everyday situations.

---

### 🎧 Listening & Shadowing

Develop pronunciation, rhythm, intonation, listening comprehension, and speaking fluency through repeated exposure and shadowing.

---

### ✍️ Production

Actively produce the language through translation, sentence creation, speaking practice, and written exercises.

---

### 🌍 Real-World Language

Connect classroom knowledge with authentic communication, culture, professional situations, travel, and everyday life.

---

### 🔄 Review

Revisit vocabulary, chunks, sentences, grammar, and conversations through continuous active recall.

---

# 🌱 Learn → Use → Repeat

> **Language becomes usable through repeated exposure, meaningful chunks, active recall, speaking, listening, and real-world use.**

### 01 · Learn in Context

Words are learned through **sentences, chunks, collocations, and situations**, not as isolated vocabulary.

### 02 · Produce Actively

Recognition is only the beginning.

Use the language by speaking, writing, translating, answering questions, and creating your own sentences.

### 03 · Review Continuously

Return to previously learned vocabulary, grammar, and expressions through repeated exposure and active recall.
